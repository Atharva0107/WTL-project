About EasyRide

Welcome to EasyRide – Your Journey, Your Way!

EasyRide is a student-developed Vehicle Rental Management System designed to demonstrate how technology can make the vehicle rental process simpler, faster, and more convenient.

The website provides a user-friendly platform where users can explore different types of vehicles, compare rental prices, estimate their rental costs, and submit booking details online.

Our system offers a selection of vehicles, including hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, premium cars, MPVs, and crossovers, to suit different travel needs. Users can view vehicle information and daily rental rates before choosing a vehicle for their journey.

Our Mission

Our mission is to demonstrate a simple, accessible, and organized digital vehicle rental process that helps users explore vehicle options and plan their travel expenses conveniently.

Our Vision

Our vision is to develop a user-friendly vehicle rental platform that demonstrates how web technologies and database management can improve the way rental services are organized.

What We Offer

  • Vehicle Browsing: Explore different vehicle categories and available options.
  • Price Estimation: Calculate estimated rental costs based on the selected vehicle and rental duration.
  • Online Booking Requests: Submit customer details and rental information through the website.
  • Easy Navigation: Access vehicle details and booking features through a simple interface.
  • Booking Management: Demonstrate how rental booking information can be stored and managed digitally.

Our Project

EasyRide is developed as an academic project to demonstrate the practical application of web development, database connectivity, and vehicle rental management concepts. It provides a simple example of how a traditional rental process can be represented through a web-based system.
